# What's New

## Version 1.40

- Acaia Pearl support, tested on hardware. Lunar, Pyxis and Cinco share the same protocol.
- Fourteen more scale models supported from their published protocols: Atomax Skale II, BOOKOO Themis, Decent Scale (plus Half Decent and EspressiScale), DiFluid Microbalance and Microbalance Ti, DIY Coffee Scale, Eclair, Eureka Precisa, Felicita Arc, IKAPE A01, INSMART 863A, Tencent (DSD TECH), TIMEMORE Black Mirror, Varia AKU and WeighMyBru. Not yet verified on a physical scale - feedback welcome.
- Scale timer in sync: START, PAUSE and RESET in the app start, stop and reset the timer on the scale.
- On Acaia, Decent and Varia scales the timer button on the scale controls the brew in the app.
