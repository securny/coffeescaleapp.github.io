# What's New

## Version 1.44

- While you pour, the left button now reads HOLD. Tap it to freeze the weight: put a lid on the brewer and the lid stays out of the weight, the brew ratio and the charts. A lock appears next to WEIGHT. Tap LIVE to see the live weight again.
- Futula and LEFU scales receive the current time right after connecting, the same way the maker's app does it.

## Version 1.43

- Lefu scales that show up as "LEFU-..." (for example LEFU-CK811) are found and connect again. Version 1.41 stopped recognizing this name.

## Version 1.42

- DiFluid Microbalance and Microbalance Ti are now tested on hardware.
- DiFluid: if the scale starts its own timer when it detects pouring, the app offers to turn that off, so the scale and the app count the same seconds.
- DiFluid: GO ON after a pause no longer clears the time on the scale display.
- The numbers at the top of the brew screen are now the same size and evenly spaced.
- The flow chart labels half steps correctly (0.5 g/s, 1.5 g/s) instead of repeating whole numbers.

## Version 1.41

- Auto-stop: lift the cup off the scale when the brew is done and the timer pauses by itself. The time and the charts end at the moment the cup left the scale.
- The timer button on BOOKOO Themis Ultra starts, pauses and resets the brew in the app. Themis Ultra is now tested on hardware.
- Flow rate now sits right next to the weight.
- The weight chart moves smoothly as you pour, without jumps.
- Numbers are dimmed until a scale is connected, so zeros never look like a measurement.
- Tap the pencil to enter the coffee dose.
- Flow rate on Acaia Pearl no longer stays at zero during the pour.
- Other Bluetooth devices, such as car diagnostic adapters, are no longer mistaken for a scale.
- TIMEMORE Dot and HOTO show MAX when the scale is overloaded, instead of a wrong weight.

## Version 1.40

- Acaia Pearl support, tested on hardware. Lunar, Pyxis and Cinco share the same protocol.
- Fourteen more scale models supported from their published protocols: Atomax Skale II, BOOKOO Themis, Decent Scale (plus Half Decent and EspressiScale), DiFluid Microbalance and Microbalance Ti, DIY Coffee Scale, Eclair, Eureka Precisa, Felicita Arc, IKAPE A01, INSMART 863A, Tencent (DSD TECH), TIMEMORE Black Mirror, Varia AKU and WeighMyBru. Not yet verified on a physical scale - feedback welcome.
- Scale timer in sync: START, PAUSE and RESET in the app start, stop and reset the timer on the scale.
- On Acaia, Decent and Varia scales the timer button on the scale controls the brew in the app.
